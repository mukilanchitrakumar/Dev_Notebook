# Authentication vs Authorization

## Question

What is the difference between Authentication vs Authorization?

## Short Answer

Authentication is the security process of verifying that users or clients are genuinely who they claim to be, typically using credentials such as passwords or SSH keys. Authorization is the separate process of determining what specific resources or operations an authenticated identity is permitted to access. In web systems, authentication establishes identity first, after which authorization rules verify permissions for every requested action.

## Simple Example

Logging into a website with an email and password is Authentication. Checking whether your account has administrator privileges to delete a post is Authorization.

## Key Point

Authentication verifies identity ('who are you?'); authorization determines permissions ('what are you allowed to do?').
